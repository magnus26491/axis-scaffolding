How Much Does Scaffolding Cost in Essex?

A straightforward guide to scaffolding prices in Essex — what affects the cost, typical price ranges for common jobs, and how to get an accurate quote from Axis Scaffolding Ltd.

The Short Answer

Residential scaffolding in Essex typically costs £350–£600 for smaller single-elevation domestic jobs and £800–£2,500+ for full roof scaffolding on larger properties. Commercial and multi-storey scaffolding is priced individually. Every job is different — the only reliable figure is a quote from a scaffolder who has assessed your specific project.

What Affects the Cost of Scaffolding?

Size and Height

More scaffold tubes, boards and fittings means more cost. A single-storey rear elevation is much cheaper than a full three-storey perimeter scaffold.

Number of Elevations

A single front-of-house scaffold is cheaper than wrapping all four sides of a property. Chimney scaffold typically involves only a small working platform.

Duration on Hire

Scaffolding is usually priced for a set hire period (often two to four weeks for domestic jobs). Extended hire increases cost — plan your trades to reduce standing time.

Site Access

Difficult access — narrow gates, slopes, restricted roads — takes longer to erect and may require specialist equipment or licences, affecting price.

Highway Licence

If scaffold extends over a public pavement or road, a Section 169 licence is required from Essex Highways. Licence fees vary by council and add to the overall cost.

Temporary Roofing

If your project needs weatherproof cover during works (e.g. roof replacement in autumn or winter), a temporary scaffold roof adds to the overall package cost.
