Housing and Properties in Thundersley

Thundersley is a largely residential area with a mix of 1930s–1960s semi-detached and detached houses across the Church Road, Kenneth Road and Rushbottom Lane areas, together with some newer developments. The housing stock is at an age where roof replacements, chimney repairs and external improvement projects are common.

Typical Scaffolding Projects in Thundersley
