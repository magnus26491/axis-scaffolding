How It Works

From your first call to final dismantling — here is what to expect when you work with Axis Scaffolding.

  1. 1

    Tell us about your project

    Call 01702 820468 or complete the short quote form. Tell us what work you are having done, your address and when you need access.

  2. 2

    We assess the requirements

    We establish property and site access, the right scaffold type, any access constraints and your timeline. We advise on highway licence requirements if relevant.

  3. 3

    You receive a clear quote

    We aim to respond to all quote requests the same working day. Your quote is clear and no-obligation — no hidden extras.

  4. 4

    We erect safely and on time

    Our CISRS-qualified team installs the agreed scaffold, working around your roofer, builder or site schedule.

  5. 5

    We dismantle and leave you tidy

    Once your work is finished, we return promptly to dismantle and remove all scaffold. Tidy handover — no materials left on site.

How Much Does Scaffolding Cost in Essex?

Residential scaffolding in Essex typically starts from around £300–£500 for smaller jobs, rising to £1,500 or more for full roof scaffolds on larger properties.

This is indicative guidance, not a quote. Your actual price depends on the factors below — we confirm it in writing after seeing the job or your photos, and it's free and no-obligation either way.

What affects the price?

  • Scaffold height and the number of elevations required
  • Footprint and overall scaffold size
  • Site access — narrow driveways, close neighbours, restricted entry
  • Whether a pavement or highway licence is required
  • Duration of hire
  • Temporary roofing requirement
